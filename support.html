Help with Stride for iPhone

Need help or found a bug? Email ovidiutoma.app+stride@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

My walk isn’t recording my route

Stride needs location access to draw your route. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Stride and choose While Using the App, with Precise Location turned on. Also check that Location Services is on.

Approximate location is too coarse to measure a walk, so Stride may ask for precise location when a walk starts.

Does it keep recording with my screen locked?

Yes. While a walk is active, Stride keeps recording with your iPhone locked and shows your progress in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Recording stops when you pause or finish the walk.

My steps don’t show on Today

Today’s steps come from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › Stride, and turn on the data types you want Stride to read, such as Steps. You can also connect from Stride › Settings › Apple Health.

Walks aren’t saved to Apple Health

In the Health app, go to Apps › Stride and allow Stride to write Workouts. New walks will be saved when you finish them.

I’m not getting reminders or milestone notifications

Check Settings › Notifications › Stride and make sure notifications are allowed. Then, in Stride › Settings, check that the daily reminder, distance milestones or records are turned on.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. In Stride › Settings you can export your walking history as JSON or CSV, or delete all data. Your walks are stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app also deletes them. Walks already saved to Apple Health stay there until you remove them in the Health app.

Will I lose my walks if I change phones?

Stride stores your walks on your device. They move to a new iPhone when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup. To keep your own copy, export your history from Settings.
