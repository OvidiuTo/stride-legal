Stride

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: Stride has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Your walks, routes and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. The developer never receives any of it.

Who we are

Stride is a walking tracker for iPhone, developed by Ovidiu Andrei Toma (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Stride app uses, why, and where it is kept.

If you have questions, contact us at ovidiutoma.app+stride@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Stride does not send any personal information to us or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, does not use third-party SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

All of the information below is created, processed and stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

DataWhy Stride uses it
Walks: start and end time, distance, duration, pace, elevation, steps, route pointsTo show your walk history, Walk Score, insights and achievements.
Preferences: goals, units, reminder time, appearanceTo remember your settings.
A small summary of today’s and this week’s activityTo show Stride’s widgets and Live Activity. It is shared only between Stride and its own widget extension on your device.

Location

Stride asks for location access while using the app. Your location is used only while you are recording a walk, to draw your route and measure distance, pace and elevation. During an active walk, location updates continue while your screen is locked so the walk keeps recording; they stop when the walk ends or is paused.

Stride does not track your location in the background when no walk is being recorded, and your location is never sent to us.

Maps in Stride are provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). To show a map, your device requests map content from Apple, which is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow it:

Stride reads

  • Steps, walking and running distance, and active energy, to show today’s activity and progress toward your goal.
  • Heart rate, to show your heart rate during a walk.
  • Workouts, only to check whether a walk has already been saved so it is not saved twice.

Stride writes

  • Completed walks as walking workouts, with distance and, where available, the route.

Health data is used only to provide these features inside the app. It is never sent to us, shared with third parties, used for advertising or marketing, sold, or stored in iCloud by Stride. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Stride or in the Health app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Stride schedules a daily walk reminder (if you turn it on) and sends notes about distance milestones and personal records during a walk. All notifications are created on your device; no server is involved.

Exports and backups

Your walking history leaves the device only when you choose to export it (as a JSON or CSV file) and decide where to save or share it.

Like other apps, Stride’s data may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual walks in the app, delete everything with Settings › Delete All Data, or delete the app. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you remove it in the Health app.

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You remain in full control of it on your device. If you live in a region with data protection rights (such as the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you can exercise those rights directly on your device as described above, and you can contact us at ovidiutoma.app+stride@gmail.com with any request or question.

Children

Stride is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Stride handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be described in the app’s release notes.
